Printed vinyl bin labels from Primo Cans - the building's address big, plus an optional extra line - designed to last 5+ years outdoors through rain, sun and road salt.

Checked against NYC & DSNY sources · Last checked November 5, 2026

What to have ready

  • The building's address as it should read on the tag (for example, "245 E 7 ST").
  • The bin count per stream - how many trash, recycling and compost bins go out on pickup night.
  • The extra line, if you want one - a flat add-on per order. Put the stream ("TRASH", "RECYCLING") or a bin number ("BIN 2 OF 3") on it; a unit or building name works too. The bulk labeling guide has the details. Need a different format entirely? Contact the shop before ordering.

Order at primocans.com, or write to info@primocans.com with the building's details and the shop will take it from there.

Why Primo Cans

Primo Cans is a Brooklyn print shop that does one thing for bins: labels that stay readable. Printed vinyl, sized for the official NYC Bin's address space, made for curbside life. The shop also runs cans.nyc, the plain-words tracker of NYC's trash-container rules, and labelyourcan.com, the labeling guide for individual homeowners.
